# PCP-01 — QNT Evidence Supplement — PRE-T0

**Status:** FROZEN SUPPLEMENT

**Purpose:** correct an evidence-completeness omission without rewriting the original frozen Evidence Registry.

## Reason for supplement

The original PRE-T0 Relationship Evidence Registry had an evidence cut of 2026-10-01 but omitted a material event published on 2026-09-24: The Clearing House selected Quant to power the On-Chain Money Initiative.

Because this evidence was available before the stated evidence cut, the omission is an execution-quality failure, not a later event. The original registry remains immutable for auditability.

## Interpretation

The supplement materially strengthens institutional validation and Causal Exposure. It does not, under frozen Economic Capture anchors, demonstrate a current mandatory or material token-transmission mechanism.

Core distinction: institutional adoption of Quant technology is not automatically Economic Capture by QNT.
