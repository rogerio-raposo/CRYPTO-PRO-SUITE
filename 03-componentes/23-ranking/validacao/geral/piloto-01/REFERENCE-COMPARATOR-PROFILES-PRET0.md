## Evidence discipline

These profiles are not full Ranking evaluations. They provide only enough comparative evidence to locate sampled Position targets inside their frozen Position Reference Universe (PRU).

Raw TVL/AUM figures are not mechanically ranked because project sources use different scopes and definitions. They are used only as supporting evidence alongside institutional footprint, production status, integration breadth, persistence and functional centrality.
