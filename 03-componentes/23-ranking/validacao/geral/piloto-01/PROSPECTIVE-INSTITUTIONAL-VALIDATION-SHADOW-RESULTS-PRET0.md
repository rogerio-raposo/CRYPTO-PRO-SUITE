## Key pattern

QNT is the only Run A asset in this shadow test with the combination:

`IV3 + PM2 + PTC1`

This pattern means:
- institutional validation is exceptionally strong;
- the deployment path is concrete but not yet live;
- token-level future capture remains weakly evidenced.

That combination is materially different from the other two PRE-T0 Materiality FAIL cases:

- ADA: lower institutional-validation strength and weak token transmission;
- ONDO: live/core vector activity but no demonstrated token transmission under the tested capture definition.

## Interpretation

The diagnostic appears to capture information that is not fully represented by the binary Materiality outcome. It distinguishes a high-significance prospective institutional commitment from generic exposure or live project activity.

However, this does not demonstrate that the diagnostic predicts institutional flow into the token, price appreciation, or superior Ranking performance.

## Decision

Do not integrate IV/PM/PTC into Run A merit or Materiality.

Retain the diagnostic for:
1. Critical Event Watch / interim monitoring research;
2. later outcome-oriented pilot validation;
3. possible future methodology revision only if distinct predictive or explanatory value is demonstrated across more than this single case.

## QNT implication

QNT remains Materiality CONFIRMED FAIL under the frozen methodology, while receiving a shadow flag for unusually strong prospective institutional validation.

This preserves both facts without rewriting the methodology around the observed market outcome.
