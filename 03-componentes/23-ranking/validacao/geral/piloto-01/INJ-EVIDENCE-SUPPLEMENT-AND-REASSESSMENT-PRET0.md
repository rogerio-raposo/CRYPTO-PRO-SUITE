## Rationale

The event does not justify Capture E3 because Stockdrop is not sufficient evidence that FV-01 activity is already a material, recurring share of total INJ economics.

It does materially improve confidence in E2 because it provides a direct, vector-specific token-transmission example linking tokenized stocks to INJ burn mechanics, on top of the already documented recurring ecosystem-revenue buyback/burn framework.
