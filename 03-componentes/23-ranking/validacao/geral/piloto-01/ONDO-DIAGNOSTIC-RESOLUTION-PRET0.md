# PCP-01 — ONDO Diagnostic Resolution — PRE-T0

**Status:** RESOLVED — no methodology defect established
**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Scope:** MGR-008 / ONDO / Economic Capture

## 1. Decision

The PRE-T0 ONDO result remains methodologically correct under the current construct definition:

`Exposure E4/C4 + Capture E1/C3 → Materiality CONFIRMED FAIL`.

Ondo's platform/franchise scale and institutional AUM are real and highly relevant to Causal Exposure and Structural Position, but they do not currently create demonstrated token-level Economic Capture for ONDO.

## 2. Current token rights

Ondo Foundation documentation identifies ONDO as the governance token for Ondo DAO and Flux Finance. The reviewed primary documentation does not establish a current revenue-share, mandatory fee, staking-yield, buyback, burn or other direct economic claim on the operating platform's revenues.

Source: https://docs.ondo.foundation/ondo-token

## 3. Fee-switch status

A June 18, 2026 community temperature-check proposed activating an automated buyback-and-burn fee switch. The post explicitly states that it is a temperature check and **not a binding on-chain proposal**.

Source: https://forum.fluxfinance.com/t/temperature-check-activating-the-ondo-fee-switch-automated-programmatic-buyback-and-burn/1138

Secondary market research describes a formal fee-switch decision as expected in H2 2026, but this audit did not verify an official binding vote schedule or executed fee-switch as of the PRE-T0 cut.

Therefore:
- fee-switch optionality may be economically relevant to market expectations;
- it is not current Economic Capture;
- it must not promote ONDO in the frozen Materiality Gate.

## 4. Classification of the prior divergence

The earlier `ONDO-MARKET-REALITY-DIVERGENCE-NOTE-PRET0.md` remains preserved for auditability, but its interpretation is narrowed.

The observed difference between platform success and token outcome is not treated as a methodology defect. It is treated as a legitimate separation between:

`platform/franchise value`
and
`current token economic capture`.

Speculation about a future fee switch belongs to prospective monitoring, not to current Materiality.

## 5. Consequence for MGR-008

MGR-008 is resolved for PCP-01:

> AUM/franchise scale alone does not satisfy Economic Capture. A future governance decision may create a new token-transmission mechanism, but until activated it remains prospective optionality.

No new construct or anchor change is authorized.
