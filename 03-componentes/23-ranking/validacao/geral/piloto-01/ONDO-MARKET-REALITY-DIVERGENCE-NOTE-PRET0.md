## 1. Reason for registration

A material tension has been identified between:

- the PCP-01 PRE-T0 outcome for ONDO (`Exposure E4/C4`, `Capture E1/C3`, `Materiality CONFIRMED FAIL`); and
- Ondo's observable market position as one of the largest and most institutionally integrated platforms in the tokenization of real-world assets, including U.S. Treasuries and tokenized securities.

This note records the tension without converting it into a methodology change or a revised ONDO state.

## 3. User-raised valuation thesis

The following is registered as a **market/value thesis to be tested**, not as an established methodological fact:

> The economic value attributed by the market to ONDO may be linked not only to currently explicit token-level cash-flow or mandatory-use mechanisms, but also to the scale, quality and institutional character of AUM and flows captured by the Ondo franchise, especially as real-world-asset and U.S.-Treasury tokenization expands.

This thesis implies a possible transmission channel of the form:

`institutional AUM / product leadership → franchise value / strategic option value → expected future governance/economic rights or ecosystem rents → ONDO valuation`

The current PCP-01 Economic Capture construct recognizes the last step only when a sufficiently explicit present token-level mechanism is demonstrated. It does not currently treat franchise scale, AUM quality, governance option value or expected future monetization as equivalent to current Economic Capture.

## 4. Why this creates a diagnostic tension

Under the frozen PCP-01 rules, ONDO correctly fails the current Materiality Gate if no present mandatory fee, buyback, burn, staking or comparable token-transmission mechanism is demonstrated.

However, the phenomenon target of the Ranking is broader than current cash-flow capture: it seeks relative positioning for **potential capture of the next institutional flow**.

If market participants rationally assign value to ONDO because Ondo controls a rapidly scaling institutional tokenization franchise with high-quality AUM and distribution, then a methodology that requires current explicit token transmission may systematically understate assets whose token value thesis is based on **prospective franchise monetization or governance option value**.

This is not yet evidence that the methodology is wrong. It is evidence of a possible mismatch between the operational definition of Economic Capture and the intended phenomenon target.

## 5. Important distinction from QNT

The ONDO case is not identical to QNT.

- QNT diagnostic: exceptional external institutional validation precedes operating deployment and current token capture.
- ONDO diagnostic: the underlying institutional/RWA franchise is already large and operating, but the ranked token's present explicit economic rights remain weakly demonstrated.

Thus QNT tests **prospective institutional materialization**, while ONDO tests **AUM/franchise-mediated token valuation**.

## 6. Anti-overfit rule

Do not promote ONDO or alter its Materiality state because:
- Ondo is a market leader in tokenization;
- tokenized RWA/Treasury markets are growing;
- the user expects ONDO to appreciate;
- the market may already price future value accrual.

Instead, treat the observed discrepancy as a hypothesis requiring symmetric testing against other Run A assets and, later, outcome-oriented validation.

## 7. Required follow-up

Register MGR-008 — AUM / Franchise-Mediated Token Value Thesis Gap.

The follow-up should test whether the methodology needs a distinct representation of:
- franchise scale and institutional-quality AUM;
- control/governance rights over a strategically important tokenization ecosystem;
- credible paths from franchise economics to future token economics;
- option value of future monetization;

without collapsing these into current Economic Capture and without using price appreciation as evidence.
