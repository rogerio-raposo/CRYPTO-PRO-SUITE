## Anchor notes

### FR-SET

E3 is reserved for networks with converging evidence of material current asset/institution footprint or specialized structural relevance. No Run A target receives E4 because the governed PRU contains several large competing networks and evidence does not establish a single structurally dominant settlement layer for FV-01.

### FR-MID

LINK receives E4 only relative to the PCP-01 FR-MID PRU. QNT is highly relevant and receives strong comparator evidence, but its major TCH deployment is not yet live while Chainlink is already embedded across multiple major institutional workflows and infrastructures.

### FR-ISS

RSR receives E2 rather than E1 because Reserve has an operating and persistent DTF ecosystem. ONDO's much larger structural platform footprint prevents E3 under the current two-member reference set.

### FR-MKT

INJ and SYRUP receive E3 for different reasons: Injective through tokenization/regulated market infrastructure; Maple through institutional credit scale. HYPE receives E2 because tokenized equities are live but remain narrower within its overall market role.
