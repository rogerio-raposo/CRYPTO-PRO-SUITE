## New event

On 2026-09-24 The Clearing House selected Quant to power its On-Chain Money Initiative. Quant is to provide the interoperability, orchestration and transaction-management layer coordinating tokenized-deposit clearing and settlement and connecting the initiative to RTP and CHIPS. The network is expected to become available to participating institutions in H1 2027.

## Reassessment — Causal Exposure

**Decision: E3 / C4.**

The event materially strengthens the case that Quant occupies an important institutional tokenized-money role. The state remains E3 because the TCH network is not yet live at the evidence cut; the announcement is a high-significance committed deployment rather than recurring operating transaction flow. Confidence rises to C4 because the proposition is direct, specific and supported by The Clearing House itself.

## Reassessment — Economic Capture

**Decision: E1 / C4.**

Current public evidence shows that QNT can be used for Quant products/services and staking, but Quant also states that platform fees may be paid in USD. The TCH announcement does not state that TCH or participant banks must buy QNT, that transaction volume creates QNT-denominated fees, or that initiative revenues are converted into, lock, burn or otherwise require QNT.

The E1→E2 boundary therefore is not crossed. Confidence rises because the limitation is supported by current direct Quant documentation rather than weak evidence.

## Materiality Gate

Confirmed PASS requires Exposure >= E2/C3+ AND Capture >= E2/C3+.

Reassessed result: Exposure E3/C4 + Capture E1/C4 = CONFIRMED FAIL.

## Controlled conclusion

After correcting the evidence omission, QNT still fails the current PCP-01 Materiality Gate. The explanation is now sharper: institutional exposure is strongly demonstrated; the binding failure is token-level Economic Capture.
