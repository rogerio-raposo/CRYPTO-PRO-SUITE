## Admission rule

`CA-PASS` requires:
1. resolved economic identity;
2. at least one current, economically testable Asset–Vector hypothesis;
3. admissible Tier 1–3 support that the relevant project/network/protocol activity is active at the as-of.

`CA-PASS` does **not** mean Materiality PASS and does not demonstrate token Economic Capture.

When evidence supports only future capability, exploratory roadmap, or a relation to a wider technology stack that is not clearly attributable to the candidate asset, the result is `CA-IND` rather than a fabricated negative conclusion.
